Do I or my team need to learn new software?

No. Your team gets one simple app on their phones that shows today’s enquiries and reminders. We set it up and train them in about 20 minutes.

Will customers know the reply is automatic?

The first reply is written in your voice and sent in your business name. It shares useful details and the next step. Your team then continues the conversation personally.

Who owns the website, the WhatsApp account and the customer data?

You do. The domain, WhatsApp Business account and CRM are registered in your name. If you ever stop working with us, everything stays with you.

What extra costs should I expect?

WhatsApp Business tools and message charges, CRM licences and your domain, usually ₹2,000 to ₹3,500 a month in total, paid directly by you. Ad budgets are separate and fully in your control.

Can I cancel?

Plans have a 6-month minimum so there is enough time to see results. After that you can stop with 30 days’ notice.

Do you work with businesses outside my city?

Yes. I work with businesses anywhere in India. Setup, training and monthly reviews all happen over video calls and WhatsApp, so your location doesn’t matter. If you’re in or near Pune, we can also meet in person.
